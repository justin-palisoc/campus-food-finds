About the Owner

Justin Palisoc

I am a student at the Technological University of the Philippines-Manila. I started this project because finding good food while staying within a student budget can sometimes be difficult. As a student, I understand that there are many expenses to think about, so finding affordable meals near campus can help students save money.

I chose this topic because I wanted to share food spots that are easy to reach, affordable, and worth trying for students. My recommendations focus on places that are convenient near campus and offer meals that can fit a student's budget. I chose these places based on their location, affordability, and how practical they are for students looking for a quick and satisfying meal.
