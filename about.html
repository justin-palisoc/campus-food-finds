About the Owner

Justin Palisoc

I am a student who created Campus Food Finds to help fellow students discover affordable and convenient food places near campus.

I started this project because, as a student, I know how hard it can be to find good food while staying within a limited budget. There are a lot of expenses like transportation, school requirements, projects, and other daily needs, so saving money is important. I wanted to create a simple guide where students can find food spots that are easy to reach, affordable, filling, and worth the money.
